Our Story

The Art of
a Graceful Goodbye

Who We Are

More Than a Sale.
A Meaningful Transition.

Sorted & Sold was founded on a simple belief: that the items accumulated over a lifetime deserve to be honored, not just dispersed. We bring together a love of beautiful things, deep market expertise, and genuine compassion to every estate we serve.

Based in the greater Pittsburgh area, our team has successfully managed hundreds of luxury estate sales — from historic Shadyside brownstones to grand Fox Chapel estates — always with the same care and attention to detail we'd bring to our own family's treasures.

We are not an auction house. We are not a clearance company. We are your trusted partner in one of life's most significant transitions.
