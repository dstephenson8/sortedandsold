Who We Are

A Fresh Start for
a Business Built on Care

Sorted & Sold was born out of a genuine belief: that estate sales should feel welcoming, well-run, and actually fun — for everyone involved. Whether you're a family navigating a difficult transition or a shopper hunting for something special, you deserve better than a chaotic, disorganized mess.

We're a new business, and we're proud of it. Being new means we're hungry, careful, and bringing fresh energy to everything we do. We don't have complacency. We have commitment.

Based right here in Pittsburgh, we know these neighborhoods, these homes, and this community. We're your neighbors — and we'll treat your estate like it's our own family's.
